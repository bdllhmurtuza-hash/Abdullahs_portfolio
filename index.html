About

A little about me.

I'm a Computer Science Engineering student in my final year, with a strong interest in machine learning and artificial intelligence. My focus is on building data-driven applications, taking a dataset and turning it into something that can predict, recommend, or make intelligent decisions.

I'm passionate about building projects that solve real problems, taking Machine Learning beyond notebooks into models that are trained, evaluated, and shipped inside real applications. I’m looking for opportunities to contribute, learn from experienced teams, and continue growing as an ML engineer.
